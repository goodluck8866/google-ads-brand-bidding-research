# Takedown notice to an affiliate, reseller or agency

[中文版](takedown-notice.zh.md)

Use this when a partner is bidding on your brand. You don't need a trademark registration for it. Every `{…}` value comes from your [evidence checklist](../checklist.md).

Four things have to be in it: **who, what, for how long, and the date it must stop by.** Don't write "you are infringing" in the first letter. Its only job is to get the ads stopped, and the more it reads as plain fact, the harder it is to stall.

---

**Subject:** Google Search ads directing traffic to our brand landing pages

Hello,

In Google's public advertising data, we can see that the advertiser "{registered advertiser name}" has been running Google Search ads pointing to {landing root domain} since {first seen date}. At least {N} of these ads have been running for more than {N} days. The most recent one we saw was on {last seen date}; we checked on {date you collected this}. Example ad IDs: {ad ID 1}, {ad ID 2}.

Under section {X} of our agreement, search advertising on our brand terms and brand landing pages is reserved to us. Please stop these ads by {date} and confirm in writing.

If a downstream partner of yours placed these ads, please tell us who they are so we can contact them directly.

{name}
{company}
{contact}
