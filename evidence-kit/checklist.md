# Evidence checklist

[中文版](checklist.zh.md)

Fill in one copy per advertiser. Fields marked **required** are what Google's [trademark policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6118) needs before it will accept a complaint. The rest make the case harder to argue with.

| Field | Required? | Where it comes from | Why it's there |
|---|---|---|---|
| Advertiser's registered name | **required** | The ad archive's advertiser page | "specific advertisers" |
| Landing page root domain | **required** | The ad's final URL | "identified on the basis of their URL(s)" |
| Your trademark number, country, Nice class | **required** | Your own registration certificate | "countries and industries … demonstrated trademark rights" |
| Screenshot of the ad, text legible | **required** for a Google complaint | The individual ad | Proves the mark is used *in the ad*, not just on the landing page |
| Ad ID | recommended | The individual ad | Lets the other side check exactly which ad you mean |
| First seen / last seen dates | recommended | The individual ad | Shows this was not a one-off |
| How long it has been running (days) | recommended | Last seen minus first seen | Separates a quick test from sustained interception |
| Countries the ad ran in | recommended | The individual ad | Must line up with the countries where you hold rights |
| Date you collected this | **required** | You | The timestamp on your evidence |

## Before you send

- [ ] The screenshot shows your mark **in the ad text**, or you are taking the contract route instead
- [ ] Every country in the complaint is one where you hold the mark
- [ ] The goods or services in the ad fall within your registered class
- [ ] The landing page is not mainly selling your genuine product or mainly giving information about it
- [ ] Screenshot, ad ID and collection date are saved together
