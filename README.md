# Google Ads brand-bidding research

[中文版](README.zh.md)

Open data and practical material on **Google Search advertising and brand bidding**, built from Google's official public ad archive. It's for brand owners dealing with someone bidding on their name, for PPC managers sizing up competitors, and for anyone who needs a citable figure.

## What's here

| | What it is | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| [**Evidence kit**](evidence-kit/) | What an accepted brand-bidding complaint contains, a field-by-field checklist, and a takedown notice you can edit and send | Brand owners, legal and partnership teams |
| [**How long does a Google Search ad last?**](research/2026-10-ad-lifespan/) | A study of 400,000 ads. Half of new search ads are gone within about four months, yet half of the ads running today have been live for nearly two years. CSV data included. | PPC managers, analysts, anyone citing numbers |
| [**Methodology**](METHODOLOGY.md) | Where the data comes from, what each field means, and what it can't tell you | Anyone using the data |

## Key numbers

From [the ad-lifespan study](research/2026-10-ad-lifespan/), sample drawn 2026-10-12:

- Median lifespan of a new Google Search ad: **121 days**
- Still running after 7 days: **92.4%** · 30 days: **74.7%** · 180 days: **40.2%**
- Median age of the ads running right now: **686 days**
- New ads from advertisers with 100+ ads in the sample: median **185 days**, against about 65 days for smaller advertisers

## What this repository won't do

- **No spend or bid data.** Google's archive has none. Any figure claiming a competitor's keyword spend is a model's estimate.
- **No per-advertiser or per-domain detail.** Studies publish shares and distributions only.
- **Not legal advice.**
